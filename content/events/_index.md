# AI for the Rest of Us: A Hands-On Workshop
## New sessions coming soon

*A live, Zoom-based session for curious beginners and lifelong learners*

Whether AI feels exciting, overwhelming, or a bit of both, this workshop meets you where you are. Over 90 minutes, we'll move past the hype and fear to explore what AI can actually do for your everyday life — practically, thoughtfully, and without any technical background required.

## What You'll Experience

- **Welcome & AI Fundamentals** — A plain-language look at what large language models are, plus a live demo.
- **The Magic Prompt Conversation Flow** — A guided exercise where you bring a personal interest and AI helps you explore it from unexpected angles.
- **Breaking News: Cell Phones Improve Longevity!** — A short, eye-opening data exercise showing how AI can help you spot misleading headlines and separate correlation from causation.
- **Self-Guided Project Time** — Choose your own adventure: plan a trip, draft a difficult email or letter, explore a different perspective on a hard topic, or start a personal project like a reading list.
- **Sandbox Time** — Free time to experiment with what you've learned.
- **Closing Reflections** — A discussion of AI's benefits, limits, and how to use it wisely.

You'll also be introduced to the **Co-Intelligence Ladder**, a simple framework for thinking about your relationship with AI — from "a system that controls you" all the way up to "a learning partner" you grow alongside.

**Format:** Live via Zoom
**Audience:** Beginners and lifelong learners welcome — no technical experience needed
**Instructor:** Mike Davis, Th.M., CWMF, Certified Mindfulness Mentor

## Before Class

Please arrive with:

- A computer with a reliable internet connection
- A working microphone, webcam, and headphones
- Zoom desktop app installed
- A free Claude.ai account (claude.ai)
