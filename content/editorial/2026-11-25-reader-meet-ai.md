If you aren't yet afraid of AI, you haven't understood it. 

This seems like hypocrisy coming from someone who has taught classes on how to use AI responsibly. Honestly, though, there is rarely a day when I don't ask myself, *Should I quit using AI altogether?* 

I'm not sure if I should quit using AI. Honestly, I think I need to use AI. How am I supposed to talk with you, my Dear Reader, if I quit? This whole website was assembled by AI, but it's my words.  Again, to be clear, while the AI made a technological frame for my words, I couldn't have assembled the frame without AI before I die. AI gives people tools they would not otherwise have. 

Therein lies the problem. Where does *human* stop and AI begin. What comes after AI? And, how much of humanity's wellbeing will be destroyed until we settle? 

I worry about all the tech-bros who are can't imagine the danger we are entering. Recently, I read an article where a media reviewer commented on a video he saw. He noted that people said the video was disturbing but, he *couldn't see it*. I can't imagine how it wouldn't be disturbing! There's no violence. There's absolute truth. And, it's utteerly cool. My wife and I couldn't get the music out of our minds. 

Everything in the video shown below -- including the woman with the bewitching eyes (added by choice)-- is AI created. The lyrics. The music. The themes. It was created by some prompts and the AI took it the rest of the way. 

If you don't yet understand AI, I want to clear that up. AI, meet my Reader. Reader, meet with AI knows about you and your world and how it hopes to *help you*.
